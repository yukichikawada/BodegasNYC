# My Bodegas Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

My Bodegas is a free iOS app for finding and sharing bodegas in New York
City. It is made by one person, has no ads, and does not sell personal data.
Two service providers, named below, process usage and crash reports on our
behalf.

## What the app collects

**Your location.** When you allow it, the app sends your current coordinates
to our server to find bodegas near you. The coordinates are used for that
query and are not stored.

**Streets Walked (optional).** If you turn this feature on, the app records
the streets you walk, including while it is in the background, so it can
highlight them on your own map. This history is kept only on your device and
is never uploaded. Turning the feature off stops recording; deleting the app
deletes the history. The feature is off by default.

**Motion (optional).** With your permission the app uses motion activity to
tell walking from riding so that cab and subway trips are not counted as
walked streets. Motion data stays on your device.

**Your account.** Signing in uses Sign in with Apple. We receive a user
identifier and the email address Apple provides, which may be Apple's private
relay address. We do not ask for your name. Browsing bodegas works without
signing in; an account is needed only to add bodegas, photos, reviews or
report problems.

**What you post.** Bodegas you add, photos you upload, reviews you write and
problems you flag are stored on our server and linked to your account.
Photos are resized and re-encoded before upload, which removes location and
camera metadata. Posted bodegas, photos and reviews are public to other users
of the app.

## Usage and crash reports

The app sends us a short record of how it is used: which features were used
and how often, which bodega was opened, the app version, and your device
model and iOS version. It also sends crash and hang reports so bugs can be
fixed. Sharing is on by default. These reports never include your precise
location or walked routes, your photos, or anything you type. When you are
signed in they carry your user identifier; otherwise they are anonymous.

Sentry handles the crash reports and PostHog the usage reports, both in the
United States. Neither is used for advertising or tracking.

You can turn this off on any device, signed in or not: tap the Account
button in the top bar and uncheck Share Usage and Diagnostics. From then on
nothing more is sent from that device and no crash is recorded. A crash is
reported only if sharing was on when it happened and still on the next time
the app opened after it.

Usage reports tied to your account are deleted from our analytics within
30 days of deleting your account. Crash reports expire after 90 days.

## Where data is stored

Account data and posted content are stored with Supabase, our hosting
provider, in the United States. Usage and crash reports are stored with
Sentry and PostHog, also in the United States. All traffic between the app
and the server uses HTTPS.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account inside the app: tap the Account button in the
top bar, choose Your Account, then Delete Account. If you signed in with
Apple, the app asks you to confirm with Apple once more, and the app's Sign
in with Apple access is revoked as part of the deletion.

Deleting your account removes your email, user identifier, photos, reviews
and flags from our server for good. Bodegas you added stay in the shared map
without any link to you. Your Streets Walked history is kept only on your
device and is not touched; clear it from the Streets Walked menu or by
deleting the app.

If you can't use the app, email the address below from the account you
signed in with and we will delete the account for you.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
data from them.
