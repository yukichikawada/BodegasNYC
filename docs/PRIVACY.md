# Bodegas NYC Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

Bodegas NYC is a free iOS app for finding and sharing bodegas in New York
City. It is made by one person, has no ads and no analytics, and does not
sell or share personal data with anyone.

## What the app collects

**Your location.** When you allow it, the app sends your current coordinates
to our server to find bodegas near you. The coordinates are used for that
query and are not stored.

**Streets Walked (optional).** If you turn this feature on, the app records
the streets you walk, including while it is in the background, so it can
highlight them on your own map. This history is kept only on your device and
is never uploaded. Turning the feature off stops recording; deleting the app
deletes the history. The feature is off by default.

**Motion (optional).** With your permission the app uses motion activity to
tell walking from riding so that cab and subway trips are not counted as
walked streets. Motion data stays on your device.

**Your account.** Signing in uses Sign in with Apple. We receive a user
identifier and the email address Apple provides, which may be Apple's private
relay address. We do not ask for your name. Browsing bodegas works without
signing in; an account is needed only to add bodegas, photos, reviews or
report problems.

**What you post.** Bodegas you add, photos you upload, reviews you write and
problems you flag are stored on our server and linked to your account.
Photos are resized and re-encoded before upload, which removes location and
camera metadata. Posted bodegas, photos and reviews are public to other users
of the app.

## Where data is stored

Account data and posted content are stored with Supabase, our hosting
provider, in the United States. All traffic between the app and the server
uses HTTPS.
